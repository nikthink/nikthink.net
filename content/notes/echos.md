### 2026-06-14
> Fuck that. I want to hike an ageless forest for hours – for indeterminate days and weeks – undisturbed by such concepts as inconvenience and dirt. I want to pause at dawn and watch the stillness of water; I want to stand in the rain and see it dance. I want to be overcome by discovery, by a newborn thought, and not because of some secret relief at gaining an edge over someone. I want to stumble sock-first into a puddle of cold filth, and laugh! I want to be free from pain. I want to be free from fleeing from pain. I want to be free from the need to accept a world of barely-mitigated catastrophes, a career of righteous triage, or a future of hierarchical glory. I want beauty, and variety, and solitude, and change.

--- mll. [Do I want to be great?](https://www.robustenough.com/p/do-i-want-to-be-great)
