### what i value

- **curiosity**.  
  every corner of the universe has something intriguing in it, even things that look simple. i could spend 80 years each on software, physics and medicine, and then want to try every profession of every new person i meet. 1000 years wouldn't be enough.
- **continuous self improvement**.  
  i care a lot about improving myself. i like introspection and honest feedback of any kind, good or bad. people should design ways of living that works with the fact that we are flawed but better when helping each other be the better version of ourselves.
- **compassion, including for the outgroup**.  
  the thing that saddens me most, week after week, is how little compassion people have for their outgroups.
- **integrity**.  
  always putting maximum effort into doing what you think is good, regardless of the circumstances.
- **liberty**.  
  i support people's right to make consensual arrangements and to pursue happiness in unusual ways.
- **being there for each other**.  
  i value consistent quality time, mutual emotional support, and committing to work through problems together.
- **focus**.  
  focusing on the few things that matter the most, at any given time.
